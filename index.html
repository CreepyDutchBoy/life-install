LIFE

v0.1 beta · cellular automaton bench · rust + slint + luajit

Conway's Game of Life with a timeline, statistics, LuaJIT rule mods and LAN multiplayer: teams, territory claimed by still lifes, combat, and a cell budget.

Install (Linux x86_64)

curlcurl -fsSL https://creepydutchboy.github.io/life-install/install.sh | sh
wgetwget -qO- https://creepydutchboy.github.io/life-install/install.sh | sh
  • installs into ~/liferust (settings in ~/liferust/cfg)
  • adds the liferust command (~/.local/bin) and an app-menu entry with icon
  • the download is SHA-256 verified; no root / sudo, no system packages touched
  • needs glibc 2.30+ (Ubuntu 20.04, Debian 11, Fedora 32 or newer)

Uninstall

local~/liferust/uninstall.sh
purge~/liferust/uninstall.sh --purge
  • removes only what the installer created; keeps your settings unless --purge
  • also available remotely: curl -fsSL https://creepydutchboy.github.io/life-install/uninstall.sh | sh

Portable

Prefer a single file? Download Life-x86_64.AppImage (sha256), chmod +x it and run. Every version is also on the releases page.
